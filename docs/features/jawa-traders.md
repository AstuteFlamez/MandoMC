# Jawa traders
Status: implemented; personal sell inventory, info item and repaired gray/red-eye rig deployed for client playtest.
Branch: bilal/jawa-traders

## Player experience
Brown Jawas roam Tatooine in neutral groups of 2–4. While a brown shop is open, navigation pauses and the Jawa looks toward its customer. Multiple customers hold the pause until the last leaves; closing, disconnecting, death or a real attack releases it. A saved pause marker restores the prior AI state after a crash/chunk reload. Each chooses exactly one configured category and sells its complete selection. Categories are saplings/plants, farming starters, greenery/dyes, ocean materials, Nether supplies, End materials, utility supplies and mineral supplies. All 61 supply offers belong to exactly one category. A persistent category ID keeps the choice stable for that entity across shop opens, chunk reloads, restarts and category ordering changes. Existing Jawas without an ID receive one on their next interaction. The shop title and Book identify the category. Right-click to browse and left-click to buy a bundle. A damaging, uncancelled attack provokes that Jawa's nearby group against the actual attacker. Cancelled hits do not provoke them. Combat closes shops. Brown Jawas retain their original ionization blaster attack, voices and death loot.

Gray Jawas are fixed salvage buyers, immune to damage, with idle looking behavior. Right-click to open a personal inventory with 27 deposit slots. Place any items in it using clicks, shift-click or drag, then press Sell to confirm the displayed total. Accepted stacks sell together; unsupported items return unchanged. Closing cancels the sale and returns deposited items plus the cursor. A Book info item explains this flow and saved returns when inventory space is unavailable. Custom items must match the exact catalog stack; ordinary material trades cannot consume custom gear or metals. Unused menu positions use the shared gray filler. Shift, hotbar, double-click and drag paths cannot extract previews. Trades require Survival/Adventure mode, proximity and no combat tag.

The first gray buyer uses the user's captured Batuu position/facing, stored only in runtime configuration. `/mmc jawa gray <id>` saves the issuing administrator's current position and facing; `/mmc jawa remove <id>` removes it. `/mmc jawa brown` summons a two-Jawa test group ahead of the administrator. `/mmc jawa status` reports service health. Additional fixed locations await the user.

## Economy and recovery
Use the existing CreditService, ledger and serialized writer; preserve existing balances. Accounts start at zero. No starter credits or changes to other reward systems are introduced. `/balance`, `/pay` and administrative `/credits give|take|set` continue to work.

Shop changes extend the existing atomic auction escrow snapshot rather than adding another balance store. Gray tray edits save inventory, deposited stacks and cursor custody as one player snapshot. Batch selling hands accepted stacks into the existing auction escrow before that snapshot is saved, then commits every stack receipt and the total credit payout together. Unsupported/unsold stacks retain their own saved return custody. Pending returns are retried on join or buyer interaction and never dropped on the ground. Partial quantities are paid proportionally to configured bundle prices, rounded down to integer cents after grouping matching quantities across slots. A purchase commits its debit and collectible item claim together. Inventory delivery and a saved player-data receipt precede claim acknowledgement. Sales save removed inventory and a recovery receipt together, then commit credit and the accepted transaction ID atomically. Save failures return unaccepted sale escrow; joins and `/ah collect` recover outstanding handoffs. A disconnected buyer's item remains claimable through `/ah collect`. Repeated transaction IDs are rejected. Gray buyback excludes ordinary iron/iron ore, diamond and leather entries; named metals and Bantha Hide are matched by catalog identity. Basic electrical salvage can also be sold. Vanilla ingredients already required by Minecraft recipes remain available through their existing routes. Original purchase prices exceed matching gray buyback prices; buyback deliberately excludes broad gear/component pools.

Initial prices and bundle sizes are configurable starting values, not a final balance target. Brown stock is organized into the eight category shops above; every item in the chosen category is available, with existing bundle sizes and prices retained. Gray buys common gathered materials and exact Agrinium/Durasteel stacks. Beskar, event components, enchanted items, potions and elytras are excluded.

## Mobs and placement
Mythic IDs: `JawaBrown` and `JawaGray`. Models: recovered `jawa_brown` and `jawa_black` (the user's gray/dark variant). Both variants now share the working brown geometry, hierarchy, UVs and animation keyframes. Gray keeps its original robe atlas and uses the brown face atlas on the restored face bone; face-only red tint and full brightness show red eyes while preserving the gray robe. Local exporter paths are removed, animation override is enabled and attack/death clips finish once. A pig body supports these custom neutral mobs even in peaceful review worlds, and avoids zombie daylight/transformation behavior. Mounting, breeding and vanilla feeding interactions are blocked. Gray anchors use persistent Mythic ownership and native invulnerability, and reattach their model on load.

Brown population checks run every 45 seconds, at most 16 loaded Jawas per configured Tatooine world. Spawn 24–64 blocks from non-spectator players, on loaded desert ground, with clear headroom and within the world border. Use native territory spawning/edit policy and keep new groups separated. Never load chunks just to spawn. Old groups expire after 20 minutes once players are farther than 48 blocks. These are configurable initial density values. Both the legacy public Tatooine and current Tatooine review dimension are configured; no worlds, map boundaries or territory zones are changed.

Before its attack pose, a brown Jawa uses the existing shared hostile-facing skill, tracks its target during wind-up, then fires the paired shots at ticks 22 and 23 of the pose, during the recoil. The complete 45-tick clip finishes; a 60-tick cadence avoids overlapping attacks. The recovered player gun retains 1.0 base damage, 18-cell magazine, 32-tick reload, 8 automatic shots/second and blue projectile. Armor wear is adapted to the supported integer value 1. Original drops use the single Mythic death-loot pipeline: 12% blaster chance plus three independent 50% single-ionized-cell rolls. Each brown Jawa additionally rolls 1–2 random parts from Wiring Coil, Basic Power Cell, Rough Power Cell, Basic Emitter Coil and Makeshift Recoil Buffer. These are real existing catalog items; advanced/event components are excluded. No duplicate console drops.

## Ownership and files
- `JawaTraders` owns placement, neutral group retaliation, anchored looking and brown supply menus.
- `JawaSellInventory` owns personal gray deposit trays, controls/info, proportional quotes, player/cursor custody and returns.
- `JawaSettings` validates population, offers, prices and complete non-overlapping category membership.
- `JawaMobs` isolates Mythic threat calls; existing `HostileFacing` handles model rotation.
- Mythic owns model attachment, AI and attack scheduling; WeaponMechanics owns gun/ammo identity and projectile damage.
- `mobs/jawas.yml` holds populations, trade content, messages and gray anchors.
- `items/catalog.yml` adds the vendor gun/ammo factories; `mobs/drops/jabba.yml` registers recovered loot with the shared adapter.
- `resource-pack/jawa-sources.json` and ModelEngine `sources.json` record asset provenance.
