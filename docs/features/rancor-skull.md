## Placement and custody

A trophy may remain floating after its clicked block is removed; there is no full-solid-floor or permanent-support requirement. Walls face outward; ceiling heads face down with player-relative cardinal rotation; existing floor orientation/geometry remains unchanged.

Normal build checks remain: current territory/clan permissions, normal world bounds/reach/game mode, an unoccupied or normally replaceable block cell, and entity collision. One barrier collision cell and one tagged display represent each placed trophy. Other blocks are not reserved or protected as supports. Legitimately break the trophy to recover its exact saved stack, including name/PDC. Explosion/piston protections apply to the trophy itself, preserving normal neighboring block policy.

A dedicated atomic journal and saved player receipts own preparation, placement and return. Full/offline inventories retain a durable return until space is available; no world-item drops are used for recovery. Schema2 stores the attachment face. Schema1 records load as floor placements without changing yaw, geometry, inventory bytes or custody. Orientation cannot change during a custody transfer. Chunk reconciliation deduplicates only this feature's markers/displays and never rebuilds over replaced terrain. Settings remain outside the item-definition installer; no external block plugin is introduced.
