# Feature: Bossk first pass
Status: first pass deployed; client playtest pending
Branch: bilal/jabba-generic-mobs

## Player experience
Fight the recovered Bossk model with its original four attack clips and Relby V-10. Bossk approaches firing range, changes position between attacks, retreats from players who crowd him, and responds to close-range opponents with a swipe → dioxis grenade → blaster combo. The grenade is an actual visible arcing item display aimed at the target's position when released; players can move away before impact. It bursts into a green gas cloud.

Gas briefly locks player position while allowing camera movement, repeatedly plays the hurt animation/sound without applying fake health damage, and applies real poison that lingers after leaving. Players regain movement to escape the remaining cloud. Bossk and all other non-player entities are excluded from cloud effects, eliminating his grenade self-damage.

## Rules and parameters
- Current baseline: 800 base health, 8 armor, 0 native contact damage, 0.32 movement speed, red boss bar; original voices, geometry and animation keyframes preserved. Attack clips finish instead of holding indefinitely; native zombie collision remains.
- Relby V-10 retains the legacy player weapon tuning: 1 base damage (down from 14), distance falloff, six-round magazine, 17-tick semi-auto shot delay (repairing the old invalid fractional full-auto rate), 80-tick reload and Standard Plasma Cells. Existing WeaponMechanics, saber and protection paths own its projectiles. Item: `/mmc give relby_v10`.
- Close combo at 0–6 blocks: swipe telegraph at tick 0, short physical lunge at tick 5 toward a target still 1.5–6 blocks away, nine-damage swipe at tick 10 if still within four blocks and sight; stop lunge momentum after the hit; grenade animation at tick 35, release at 45; firing animation at 53, six shots scheduled at 63–64, 67–68 and 71–72. Total busy pose 100 ticks. Combo cooldown rolls 8–11 seconds.
- At 6–32 blocks, independently varied 3–5-second decision cooldown chooses burst, grenade or aimed shot. Dedicated grenades have an additional 7–10-second cooldown. Delayed effects recheck caster/target life, distance and sight. Named busy auras prevent overlapping attacks and restore AI automatically.
- Navigate toward 13-block range, retreat from players within four blocks, and choose nearby 3–6-block surface positions while fighting at 5–18 blocks. Reposition at most once per second. Combat navigation is 1.2x ordinary speed; walk playback is unchanged. Losing combat clears tactical movement.
- Dioxis settings in `bossk.yml` require restart: 16-tick fixed-destination arc with configurable 0.65-block lift above its straight path, 120-tick cloud, four-block radius, 30-tick position lock, 100-tick immunity between locks, 100-tick poison I, cosmetic hit every five ticks. Bounded validation prevents permanent control or unbounded hazards. At most two active grenades/clouds per Bossk and 32 globally.
- Cloud exposure is player-only, Survival/Adventure only, bounded vertically and blocked by terrain. Existing safe zones deny grenade travel/exposure; poison owned by this ability is denied inside safe zones. Clouds never break blocks or emit explosion damage.
- Poison applications and poison ticks refresh the existing combat tag. Cosmetic feedback never calls a damage method. Movement/death/logout/teleport/disable cleanup releases locks; source death/removal expires active hazards. Natural poison duration remains the lingering effect after escape.
- The 50% Relby drop remains. Bossk additionally drops 1–3 usable catalog Dioxis Grenades on every actual death; removal/despawn does not roll loot. The old vendor explosion grenade is replaced by the shared player-only cloud implementation. Remaining legacy ammo loot is deferred.

## Smallest playable version

## Ownership and integrations
MythicMobs owns targeting, boss stats, movement decisions and combo/animation timing. ModelEngine renders the preserved rig. WeaponMechanics owns the Relby player item and bullets. MandoMC owns grenade flight, cloud exposure and player movement locks. A console-only UUID bridge (`mandomc bosskgrenade`) is invoked by the Mythic release skill; it revalidates the actual Bossk identity, target, range, sight, world and protection before launching. Players cannot invoke this internal route.

Source assets are recorded in `resource-pack/bossk-sources.json` and model provenance. Blueprint source is unchanged except sanitized local paths/ID and once-only attack clips. Pack media are recovered from the existing pinned archive. No new pack dependency is introduced.

## Animation flow and usable grenade (2026-11-09)
This section supersedes earlier shot-count/timing descriptions. Attack1 is the lunging swipe, attack2 emits exactly three shots, attack3 launches the grenade, and attack4 emits one aimed shot. Grenades originate at the animated `rifle` bone's barrel tip, using the inverse bind-space offset of the original model. The release plays a dispenser-launch thump plus a low firework-launch sound. No model keyframes or geometry are changed.

After release, the grenade travels for 16 ticks. At release +12 ticks, Bossk ends the launch pose and quickly navigates 3–5 blocks away at 1.8x speed. After 20 ticks of movement, he plants in attack4 and fires once after its 15-tick wind-up. The shot is therefore about 31 ticks after normal impact, allowing the cloud to expose/stun/poison before the shot. A separate combo gate prevents unrelated attacks or ordinary reposition requests interrupting these stages. Close combo fires around tick 93; standalone grenade sequence around tick 58. Native collision/navigation remain authoritative.

Bossk health is now 800. A confirmed, uncancelled damaging swipe applies outward/upward velocity (1.5 horizontal, 0.5 vertical) on the next server tick. A two-tick Mythic swipe marker distinguishes melee from Relby projectiles. Cancelled/blocked hits and protected players receive no knockback.

`dioxis_grenade` is a native catalog item with the original legacy grenade inventory/hand artwork, available in `/mmc items` and `/mmc give dioxis_grenade`. Right-click in the main hand to throw: Survival/Adventure consumes one only on successful launch; Creative does not consume. A one-second per-player cooldown and two-active-hazard per-owner cap prevent spam; rejected launches do not consume items. The throw follows the aimed block/nearby ground. Its cloud uses the same radius, short stun and poison as Bossk's, excludes its thrower, respects protected zones and tags both the attacker and exposed victims for combat. Death drops are supplied once through Mythic's actual death list as a single 1–3-item stack. New defaults are in `items/dioxis.yml`, installed additively on startup.

## Aim, facing, muzzle and cloud polish (2026-11-09)
Latest behavior supersedes the earlier aim/particle/timing notes. Attack4 now has its own zero-spread NPC shot path, sampling target movement during wind-up and predicting a straight-line intercept (2.75 blocks/tick Relby speed; at most ten ticks of lead). It aims at the upper body and revalidates source/target life, range, LOS and protection. Damage stays at the accepted nerfed value of 1. Attack2 remains three shots and attack4 one shot.

Bossk turns before beginning the attack clip, with six ticks of preparation and bounded 30-degree body turns per update. Native rotation/controller and visible ModelEngine body/head are synchronized; tracking continues during the wind-up and the swipe rechecks the target before lunging and connecting. Planted phases allow rotation. This prevents the old instant half-turn inside a newly started animation. Preparation increases total combo durations while preserving the grenade exposure delay and movement stage.

A non-rendering `bossk_muzzle` locator is added under the original rifle rig at its barrel tip. Model/animation keyframes and visible geometry are unchanged. Gameplay resolves the current animation chain before querying the locator, including when ModelEngine skips full transforms for a model with no viewers. Attack3 releases at its 0.85-second recoil cue (tick 17). The entire regenerated client pack is byte-identical to the published pack, so no new download is needed for this marker.

The shared dioxis radius is now two blocks (was four). Green DUST gas is mixed with Minecraft's actual CLOUD particle; no white DUST is emitted. Both the boss and usable grenade share this reduced radius. Gas movement lock now cancels the positional move with an anchored return position while allowing camera input, rather than changing the destination and creating a teleport correction. This avoids combat-travel warnings from the stun itself; voluntary combat travel restrictions are unchanged. Paper documents cancellation's event-free correction in [PlayerMoveEvent](https://jd.papermc.io/paper/26.1.2/org/bukkit/event/player/PlayerMoveEvent.html).
