# Feature: Darth Vader’s Lightsaber
Status: throw accepted by user on 2026-11-08; blocking/stamina follow-up deferred
Branch: bilal/vader-lightsaber

## Player experience
`/mmc give vader` issues one shield-based Darth Vader lightsaber; it also appears in Weapons in the admin browser. The hilt appears in GUI/ground contexts, the red blade when held, and the original JustTimm blocking pose on use. Holding/deselecting plays legacy ignition sounds. Item lore follows the accepted badge/blank-line/description/yellow-keybind standard.

## Rules
Main-hand attack uses native attack attributes and charge. Right-click blocks; incoming front-facing E11 bolts and arrows reflect toward the defender’s aim. No timed parry. Side/rear bolts do not reflect. Each block/reflection consumes guard heat; overheat clears use and disables defense/attack/throw for the configured interval, then restores the guard. Heat is player-owned, independent of durability, so changing sabers or repair cannot reset it. Native shield wear remains separate; enchanting is now disabled and ordinary repair remains available in the combat-basics slice. Offhand saber use is denied.

Sneak + attack launches a returning saber. The hand and original inventory slot are empty during flight. The flight reserves the exact item, including its metadata; player persistent data stores its serialized item and original slot so a saved airborne state can recover on join. Catch, slot change, teleport, logout and shutdown return that item once. Inventory transfers/drops/swaps and pickups are blocked during flight. If another plugin fills the slot, the saber returns elsewhere in inventory, or becomes an owner-bound drop if inventory is full. Normal death adds the airborne saber to the death drops; keep-inventory death returns it to inventory. Login also normalizes legacy hilt presentation from earlier builds.

The throw now follows one analytic oval with a continuous circulation direction. The user rejected the previous crossing path as a figure-eight and requested a rounder/circular sweep. A modest baseline width gives the ends room to curve while keeping the oval compact relative to its reach. An assisted throw rotates the complete oval so the outward leg intersects the chosen mob; it does not pull a waypoint across the return leg. Vertical variation lifts the recovery, and smooth target/hand correction preserves continuous motion. Per-launch distance and curvature tables retain gradual braking through bends and acceleration afterward.

The accepted 18-block reach, spin coefficient 38, angle-driven tilt and substantial per-throw variation remain. Each launch samples width, rise, recovery height, bank strength, spin and tempo; 30% mirror the usual side at full variation. Tilt follows travel slope and signed turning angle, with damped quaternion interpolation capped at six degrees per tick for the spin plane. Spin remains independent of tilt. The banking approach was informed by [Craig Reynolds' steering paper](https://www.red3d.com/cwr/steer/gdc99/); the saber's path and tuning are our implementation.

The initial aim selects one visible, damageable mob nearest the crosshair inside an 18-degree half-cone, excluding the owner's tame pet. Tracking corrects at most 0.15 blocks per tick within 1.5 blocks of its original position, then drops without retargeting if obstructed, dead or too far away. Tracking also stops on impact or after the attacking crossing, so the saber does not chase a knocked-back mob. Close targets shorten the loop. Without a target it follows an oval oriented to release aim. Vertical aim remains supported.

Render and collision share a centered, rotating two-block blade pose derived from the pinned upstream fixed-context model. Translation and rotation are subdivided by maximum tip travel (0.12 blocks); hit tests cover the complete blade segment plus the configured thickness. A pass can hit multiple nearby victims. Both legs damage on each separate pass; spinning while the same victim remains within the blade's reach counts as one sustained contact. Leaving that reach and crossing again permits another hit. Each contact temporarily bypasses the previous native hurt window while preserving its prior state and Bukkit damage cancellation. Accepted hits play the existing impact sound and a short critical/spark burst; native hurt flash and death effects remain. Paper's cancellable knockback event redirects the native horizontal impulse along the blade's travel, preserving resistance and vertical response. Cancelled damage produces no custom hit effect.

The blade and its travel check blocks, protected areas and loaded chunks before damage. Permission samples are at most 0.25 blocks apart, with block-coordinate results shared within one move. A wall or denied endpoint triggers direct recall; a blocked recall restores the existing item without crossing the obstruction. No terrain edits or chunk generation are requested. Flight lifetime remains bounded to 100 ticks.

Combat is denied in SpawnWorld and the survival landing, including throw starts, flight endpoints, victims and reflected-shot destinations. Bukkit damage events preserve vendor/future clan cancellation; clan-specific behavior remains deferred. WeaponMechanics retains responsibility for E11 damage and final hit processing.

## Smallest playable version
One Vader saber, one E11 interaction, one configured heat meter and a returning throw. Tuning is provisional, not final gear balance. Crafting, other saber variants, Force powers and global removal of ordinary weapons remain future work.

## Ownership and integrations
MandoMC owns catalog identity `vader` / `mandomc:item_id`, item attributes, heat and flight lifecycle. WeaponMechanics 4.3.1 owns projectile simulation/damage. Its immutable shooter field requires constructing a new reflected projectile, not simply changing the old bolt’s direction. The public API adapter preserves weapon configuration/speed and bounds reflection chains; the defender owns the new bolt.

JustTimm owns the artwork. His terms permit server use but prohibit rehosting extracted assets without permission. The original version 3.2.2 ZIP is downloaded by clients from its official Modrinth CDN on player join, with a deduplicated pack-loaded fallback. Its SHA-1 and URL are pinned in sabers.yml and dependencies.json. We reference only the three Vader model entry points. The upstream pack has no font/shader overrides; its named-item shield selection does not control our explicitly assigned item_model. Credits and thanks are included in resource-pack/src/credits.txt and the pack README.

Legacy improvements: directional reflection instead of omnidirectional protection; correct reflected-shot owner; guard independent of wear; bounded shared scheduler instead of a task per throw; one reserved item with persisted ownership and exact return; safe-zone checks on each damage path. Content is config, vendor APIs stay in integrations, and no parallel item registry is introduced.

## Parameters
`items/sabers.yml` owns name, lore, model, rarity, native attack-damage (10.5) and attack-speed (1.0). `sabers.yml` owns behavior: throw damage 8 per pass, cooldown 40 ticks, range 18 blocks, nominal outward speed 1, return cruise/recall cap 1.5, blade hit thickness 0.22 blocks, arc width coefficient 1.8, launch height coefficient 0.65, return height coefficient 2.4, bank limit coefficient 25 degrees, spin coefficient 38 degrees/tick (previously 42), target half-angle 18 degrees, tracking correction limit 1.5 blocks and variation 1.0. Variation is configurable from 0 (fixed style) to 1 (full range); the existing schema receives additive defaults.

At full variation, width scales 0.55–1.25×, launch rise 0.15–1.8×, recovery height 0.55–1.45×, bank limit 0.45–1.5× and spin 28.5–53.2 degrees/tick. Shape coefficients control the oval span and vertical variation, not literal maximum offsets; width/height shrink at close range. The oval has no crossing or separately stitched turn segment, and curvature-aware timing spreads tight turns over multiple ticks. Its half-width is 0.06 × reach + 0.85 × sampled width, capped to 0.3 × reach; the complete three-dimensional curve is scaled to remain within reach. Tempo varies 0.88–1.15×. Per-launch distance and curvature tables drive progress, with gradual speed changes through bends and at the catch; the old fixed-duration curve clock and return catch-up clamp are removed. Return speed is a nominal cruise divided by tempo, while emergency recall uses it as a strict cap. Flight lifetime remains 100 ticks. The new optional `throw-spin-degrees` key defaults to 38 for existing configs; the installed local range was explicitly updated to 18.

Guard capacity 100, cost 20 per hit, cooling 0.5/tick after 30 ticks idle, overheat 80 ticks; reflection half-angle 80 degrees and chain limit 4. `messages.yml` owns feedback. Item content uses `/mmc items reload`; behavior and dependency pins require restart. Existing inventory items are not automatically rebalanced. No new artwork or resource-pack release is needed for this motion change.

Adding item families now installs missing bundled rarity/category badge keys without overwriting operator choices. The existing robes remain unchanged.

## Deferred blocking and stamina
The user explicitly deferred blocking and reflection work until the first mobs that shoot blasters are available. Existing guard/reflection code is a provisional baseline, not client-validated blocking. After that blocking work is implemented and validated, saber throws should consume a large amount of saber stamina. The amount remains undecided; this path/item fix does not add a throw cost or new hostile mobs.
