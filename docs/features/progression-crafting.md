## Desert natural-spawn boundary
The pinned 26.1.2 biome implementation lists Rabbit, Camel, Husk and Parched as desert-specific candidates. Husk initialization can also create Camel Husk/rider companions before its late spawn event. The blacklist therefore retains SLIME and adds HUSK, RABBIT, CAMEL, CAMEL_HUSK and PARCHED globally, without adding generic zombies/pigs/skeletons/creepers.

The existing late `CreatureSpawnEvent` guard handles NATURAL and the legacy CHUNK_GEN reason; Paper's early `PreCreatureSpawnEvent` also aborts natural candidates before Husk cavalry initialization. Breeding, custom/command, spawner/egg, event and conversion reasons are not broadly cancelled. Existing sandy replacement remains at HIGH and its independent bounded encounter tick remains unchanged; custom model hosts are PIG/ZOMBIE and custom summons do not use the natural reason.

Modern CHUNK_GEN events are no longer fired: initial worldgen entities already exist in the new chunk. The [pinned Paper API](https://jd.papermc.io/paper/26.1.2/org/bukkit/event/entity/CreatureSpawnEvent.SpawnReason.html#CHUNK_GEN) recommends checking new-chunk load state instead. The new handler suppresses only initial blacklisted DEFAULT/NATURAL entities in `ChunkLoadEvent.isNewChunk()` chunks, without loot. It skips existing chunks, explicit spawn reasons and named/PDC/scoreboard-tagged entities. It does not sweep existing creatures. This applies to chunks generated while the plugin is active; final-world initial generation must be prepared with the policy active rather than generating an initial population before plugin startup.

Exact server evidence: inspected the pinned `BiomeDefaultFeatures.desertSpawns`, `Husk.finalizeSpawn`, `NaturalSpawner` early-event ordering and `WorldGenRegion.addFreshEntity` behavior. The latter serializes initial entities directly without the normal spawn callback. The 928-assertion Paper pass covers every SpawnReason, actual denied natural spawns, explicit command/custom/spawner/breeding survival, fresh-vs-existing chunk handling and all five real Mythic sandy summons.

## Pending patrol design — excluded from this release
The live override was already one encounter opportunity every 10 seconds (`spawn.chance: 1`, 32 placement attempts), while shipped defaults remain 20 seconds, chance 0.25 and 8 attempts. Preserve those existing interval/chance/attempt values. The old selector chose uniformly among patrol/bantha/dewback/massiff, so patrol chance per opportunity was chance/4. Pending `spawn.patrol-frequency-multiplier: 3.0` raises only the free patrol opportunity to min(1, chance/4*3). Wildlife independently retains chance*3/4, split equally among the same three species. With live values that is 0.25 -> 0.75 patrol chance (1.5 -> 4.5 theoretical patrol opportunities/minute while free); wildlife remains 0.75. Busy patrol slots and protection/placement/population checks still suppress creation.

`PatrolState` is the sole main-thread association writer. Every patrol member, including companions, resolves to one persistent encounter group ID. Each player reserves at most one active group. Nearby unassigned players share an existing group; separate players can have independent groups. Actual combat targets take priority over proximity when recovering associations. Conflicting new target assignments are rejected, and previously conflicting native targets are cleared without forcing a replacement target. The pinned Mythic PlayersGoal delegates to native NearestAttackableTargetGoal, so its reason-bearing native target events use this guard. Creative immunity/normal target eligibility remain native.

A reservation lasts while an eligible player is within the configured 96-block population radius of hostile members or is their actual target. After 30 seconds (`patrol.disengage-seconds`) without either condition it releases on a population pass; with a 10-second live interval the check is bounded by that interval. All hostile members must be gone to defeat the patrol; surviving passive banthas do not keep combat reservations indefinitely. Death/removal and logout/player-death/world-change/Spectator transition clear relevant associations. Releasing a player never despawns another player's group. Existing no-player-within-128 natural cleanup remains unchanged. Loaded group/kind PDC reconstructs membership after restart/unload; transient player claims recover from current targets/proximity.

Group size (2–4 Tuskens, optional banthas/massiffs, six total), levels 1–5, accepted biomes, 32–64 spawn distance, 32 separation and 12-nearby/96-world limits remain. New creation also checks the intended nearby cap around the candidate player as well as each site. No patrol can bypass these caps to satisfy the higher opportunity rate.

Deterministic 100,000-opportunity comparisons using the actual selector: at live chance 1, legacy patrols 25,171 -> 75,029; wildlife 74,829 -> 74,806. At shipped chance 0.25, patrols 6,285 -> 18,730; wildlife 18,819 -> 18,508. These are selector trials, not production-world rate claims.
