# Feature: Native clans, raids and land restoration
Status: deployed; client playtest pending
Branch: bilal/native-clans

## Player experience
`/c create <name>`, `/c invite <player>` and `/c join <clan>` establish a clan of at most four members. Invitations expire after five minutes. The leader can kick, transfer leadership, claim/unclaim the current chunk and disband. Members can leave, read `/c info [clan]` and `/c warpoints <rival>`, and use `/c home` through the shared travel warmup/combat gate. `/c sethome` requires safe standing space inside an owned claim. Death respawn prioritizes a valid clan home, then the existing bed/spawn flow. `/c map` overlays purple claims with owner hovers, including a nearby map for previously unmapped worlds. Existing aliases remain.

## Rules
A clan owns at most four full-height chunks across all worlds. After the first claim, a new claim must share a cardinal edge with existing clan land in the same world. Ordinary unclaim cannot increase disconnected components; old disconnected claims are retained, and /c unclaimall or disband can clear whole territory. A rival claim or static safezone/warzone must have a full intervening chunk, including diagonals. Static protection and world borders take priority; the preserved Hoth policy currently has no claimable wilderness. Clanmates cannot damage each other, including attributed projectile, weapon and vehicle damage. Native WeaponMechanics and ordinary Paper damage hooks enforce the same rule.

Each rivalry starts at zero. One opposing clan kill adds one to the killer's side and subtracts one in the reverse view; unrelated pairs are unchanged. Non-player, unaffiliated and same-clan deaths do not count. Existing combat-logout killer attribution goes through the same death listener. KOTH/Jabba PvP counts.

At +10, a clan can explosively breach its rival's claims and open accessible containers. The first successful explosion starts a fixed one-hour raid, consumes that pair's score to zero and freezes scoring between those two clans for the hour. Other rivalries still score normally. Further explosions never extend the hour. The defending clan can be offline and retains its claims. After expiry, a fresh +10 is required. This supersedes the old continuation-window design. Hand breaking, bucket/tool grief, unrelated container access, cross-border automation and unattributed explosions cannot bypass ownership. The existing bounded explosion engine determines eligible blocks/radius/caps; containers, indestructible blocks and several compound blocks remain excluded. Entity damage is independent of permission to damage terrain. Claim breaches do not join the short-lived temporary blast-hole journal.

After seven elapsed days without a member online, the clan enters an irreversible public-loot period. A server-wide announcement names the clan and all claimed world/block coordinates. Claims remain owned for 24 hours; every player can open containers, operate access controls and use attributed explosives to reach loot. Public looting does not permit unrestricted hand breaking. A returning member cannot save the clan or recruit/claim/unclaim/disband to alter the deadline. After 24 hours, membership and claims are removed, the land becomes wilderness and forced gradual restoration starts; new edits/claims cannot postpone cleanup. Manual unclaim/disband instead begins the normal 24-hour wilderness grace period.

Wilderness tracks the latest Survival edit **per chunk**, not per individual block. Each edit restarts that chunk's 24-hour elapsed timer. Claims suspend wilderness restoration. Buckets, tools, multiple-block placements and propagation across chunk borders are included. Original terrain is now prepared in an explicit frozen batch over finite generated wilderness. Player edits/claims do not sample terrain: prepared claims succeed immediately, unsupported/unfinished chunks are denied clearly. See [frozen wilderness](frozen-wilderness.md). Imported maps are restored from snapshots, never regenerated from their seed. Earlier edits made before this feature first captured an area cannot be reconstructed. Baselines store block data, not inventories or loot: restoration never replenishes looted original containers. Explicit Creative staff edits in tracked cells are excluded.

Restoration scans upward with one global scan budget and a block-change budget, removes player blocks and restores mined terrain without drops, and emits cloud particles. It waits rather than putting solid blocks inside living entities. Only loaded chunks animate; overdue unloaded chunks resume when loaded. Progress, deadlines, staff exclusions and compressed original terrain survive restarts. Baselines remain on disk and load into memory only for capture/restoration. Snapshot compression and lifecycle persistence use one IO worker. Failures fail closed for edits instead of silently losing the reset baseline.

## KOTH and crate reward
Contestant identity is the stable clan ID, with unaffiliated players remaining solo contestants. A clan retains the three-minute timer while any eligible member remains on the hill; enemy presence alone does not reset it. At completion, one eligible winning member **currently inside the hill** is chosen uniformly and receives one saved virtual KOTH key. Clan and recipient are announced publicly. Solo winners receive their own key. Absent, dead, vanished, Creative, spectator and invulnerable players cannot receive it. Rewarding ends capture before delivery to prevent a failed save from retrying a second award on the next tick.

`/crates preview koth`, `/crates keys`, `/crates open koth` and `/crates claim` use the same CrateService/CrateDefinition/WeightedPool implementation as the concurrent Kyber crate work. KOTH defines one weighted roll: 35% sixteen Durasteel Sheets; 30% twenty-four Agrinium Sheets; 20% four Beskar Scraps; 10% four Phrik Sheets; 5% four Beskar Sheets. The key uses the existing Wayfarer key model. No new resource pack is needed. The shared engine owns animation, fixed reward selection, key consumption and pending delivery. Native player PDC saves virtual keys and pending rewards. Full inventories do not lose keys/rewards.

## Ownership and integrations
ClanState owns membership, leaders, claims, homes, activity, abandonment and pairwise raids. ClanStorage owns its versioned YAML representation. LandRestoration owns compressed immutable chunk baselines and small atomic progress metadata. TerritoryPolicy remains the static safezone/warzone authority. ClanProtection adds ownership enforcement without uncancelling existing denials. TemporaryExplosions retains ownership of short-lived wilderness blast holes; claim breaches are excluded. CombatService remains the sole combat tag/logout owner; TeleportService remains the sole voluntary travel gate. KOTH resolves clan contestants and delegates one key to the shared crate engine.

Bootstrap adds the missing `crates.koth` definition to an existing crate file instead of replacing its Kyber definitions. A one-time `clan-rewards-version: 1` migration enables the previously deferred KOTH reward; subsequent operator reward overrides remain respected. CommandBindings owns ClanFeature, retaining the optional IlumFeature lifecycle first. ClanFeature merges KOTH configuration/key definitions synchronously before deferred Ilum initialization, then reuses its one registered CrateService; a standalone crate owner is created only when Ilum is absent. Ilum inventory escrow also retains respawn priority. The scoped overlay preserves the live MandoPlugin bootstrap, existing crate engine, Jawas, recipes, pack and every unrelated entry byte-for-byte. Do not deploy this worktree's whole JAR over unrelated active feature overlays.

## Parameters
`clans/settings.yml` schema 1: max-members 5; max-claims 4; raid-warpoints 10; raid-seconds 3600; inactive-seconds 604800; public-loot-seconds 86400; wilderness-reset-seconds 86400; restore-blocks-per-step 24 every five ticks; friendly-fire false. `language/clans.yml` owns clan messages. `crates/crates.yml` owns reward weights/items. Changes require restart. Clan lifecycle data and land baselines are runtime-only, outside Git.
